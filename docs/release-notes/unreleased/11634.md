# Bug Fixes

* lnd now [requests a five-minute overlap for live gossip
  updates](https://github.com/lightningnetwork/lnd/pull/11634) to reduce the
  chance of missing recent channel updates during graph sync. Previously, an
  update signed before live gossip began could arrive at a peer too late for
  its sync reply and then be excluded by its live gossip filter. Missing a
  channel update can leave lnd without the policy it needs to route in that
  direction. As a side effect, peers may resend up to five minutes of gossip
  whenever a gossip sync with them starts or rotates, which shows up as a
  burst of stale updates in debug logs.
