# Bug Fixes

* lnd [no longer misses channel
  updates](https://github.com/lightningnetwork/lnd/pull/11634) that a peer
  receives while it answers our gossip sync queries. When we start receiving
  live graph updates from a peer, we now ask for updates signed up to 10
  minutes in the past. Before, such an update could be left out of the sync
  and filtered out of the live updates too, so we never learned that half of
  the channel's policy and couldn't route through the channel.
