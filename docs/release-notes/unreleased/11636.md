# Technical and Architectural Updates

## BOLT 12 (Offers)

* [Settle BOLT 12
  invoices](https://github.com/lightningnetwork/lnd/pull/11636): a node now
  settles payments to its BOLT 12 offers. The first HTLC of an invoice carries
  the invoice envelope, and the node checks it against the payment path id,
  rebuilds the invoice, stores it and settles it. An envelope amount below the
  offer price does not settle. The invoice RPCs show the new `bolt12_detail`
  field for BOLT 12 invoices.

  `SubscribeInvoices` sends the Open event of a BOLT 12 invoice when its first
  HTLC is accepted, not when the node issues the invoice. After an MPP timeout,
  the node deletes the invoice, and a subscriber that already got its Open
  event gets no further event.
